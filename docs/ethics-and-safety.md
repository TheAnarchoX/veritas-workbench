# Ethics and Safety

Veritas Workbench is designed for truth-seeking, falsification, and provenance. It must not become a harassment, doxxing, stalking, or deanonymization tool.

Rules:

- Do not implement face recognition for identifying private people.
- Keep any person identity notes optional, user-provided, and separate from evidence.
- Do not build workflows that infer home addresses, private identities, private associations, or other doxxing targets.
- Avoid inflammatory wording. Use careful language such as "supports", "is consistent with", "does not establish", "inconclusive", and "requires original source".
- Findings need confidence, evidence, limitations, and a falsification path.

The app distinguishes:

- media appears AI-generated
- media appears AI-altered
- media provenance is missing
- account/persona may be constructed
- person does not exist

The last claim requires strong, independent evidence and should not be inferred from media artifacts alone.

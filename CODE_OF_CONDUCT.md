# Code of Conduct

Veritas Workbench is intended for careful OSINT and media provenance work. Contributions must keep the project useful, lawful, and safe for investigators, researchers, journalists, and maintainers.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, factual, and specific in issues, pull requests, and reviews.
- Challenge technical claims with evidence and reproducible examples.
- Keep user safety, privacy, and consent in scope when proposing features.
- Use cautious language around identity, attribution, authenticity, and AI-generation claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or sustained disruptive behavior.
- Doxxing, stalking, deanonymization workflows, or requests to identify private people.
- Contributions that enable bypassing access controls, platform terms, robots restrictions, paywalls, anti-bot systems, or privacy safeguards.
- Overstated detector-style claims, especially claims that a single heuristic proves AI generation, manipulation, or attribution.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close, hide, or remove contributions that violate this policy. Serious or repeated violations can lead to blocked participation in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the security/contact channel listed in `SECURITY.md`.
